PUNCH

Punch was born at Ichikawa City Zoo in Japan in July 2025. His mother wasn't able to care for him, so the keepers raised him. Because baby macaques instinctively cling to their mothers for warmth, safety and development, the keepers tried different soft objects and Punch chose a little orangutan plushie and netizens named that plushie "Oramama" (so cute T.T) Oramama became his little source of comfort. However the beautiful part is that Punch is slowly growing beyond it and has started interacting with the other monkeys and making connections of his own.
